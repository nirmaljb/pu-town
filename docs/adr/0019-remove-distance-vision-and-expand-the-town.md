# Expand the town and separate Vision from Hearing

Use a 7680 × 4320 vibrant village, nine times the previous area, with horror and cyberpunk map alternatives. Preserve the starting village geometry at the centre, with four separate interiors, and distribute additional Tasks through the surrounding districts. The first drafts used verified CC0 sources. On 5 October the owner explicitly supplied `tilesets.rar` and requested its use across all themes. Detailed supplied sprites replace the earlier exterior art; no license is inferred for that archive. Village now has ten furnished interiors, and the alternatives six each. Scenery animation does not change authoritative collision or recipient visibility.

Remove the distance-based part of ADR 0016's shared Vision and the client fog. Living Participants see living Participants in their own area at any distance; interiors, Ghost concealment, private Roles and Night results remain enforced by the server. Hearing remains proximity- and area-limited under ADRs 0017–0018. This trades local visual uncertainty for unrestricted camera visibility in a larger town without widening conversations or private Game views.

Automatic Role Setup uses one Mafia for four to six Memberships, two for seven to nine and three for ten, with one Doctor and one Sheriff. A Host can override the counts in a side overlay that does not resize the canvas. Disconnected reservations count; manual overrides persist through membership changes, and Start fixes the deal. The existing four-Player minimum and Villager requirement remain.
