# Isolate Day voice by speaker

Use one self-hosted media room per Day speaker, with publication only in the
speaker's primary grant and a separate receive-only grant for each authorized
listener. Accepted movement and area changes revoke each pair independently;
hidden listening identities prevent one listener's presence extending another's
hearing or revealing distant listeners. This avoids trusting client subscriptions
or modifying every SFU subscription signaling variant, at the cost of up to ten
media connections per browser (one publisher and nine listeners) and up to one
hundred concurrent connections for ten Players. A native-client full-mesh proof
verified all ninety authorized audio/activity pairs and independent movement
revocation; see [the final verification record](../verification/2026-10-05-final-acceptance.md).
Ten-browser resource use and physical audio remain unverified.
