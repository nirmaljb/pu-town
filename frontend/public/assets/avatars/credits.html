Character art credits

PU Town's six Avatar Presets use the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator artwork. Thank you to all contributing artists.

Modifications: skin, hair and clothing palette substitutions, then layered composition. Original frame geometry is preserved. Artwork is used under OGA-BY 3.0 where available, and CC0 for the bob and long straight hairstyles.
