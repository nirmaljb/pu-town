# Retired campus authoring evidence

Kalapet Main Campus is retired from playable/native content. Its canonical
layout, route report and `tools/campus/publish.py` publisher have been removed;
`campus` is no longer an accepted Town Map ID. The current native client uses
**Woodland Village** and **Market Town**. Their canonical geometry and seeded
TownGeneratorOS provenance live under `towns/`; see [the native guide](../native/README.md)
and [town authoring](../tools/towns/README.md).

`assets/` preserves historical licenses, source inventories, editable-master
registration metadata and review evidence. The retained Quaternius Nature and
neutral Props inventory also supplies selected assets reused by the current
towns, as recorded in [the active source inventory](../towns/assets/sources/README.md).
These historical paths do not expose a campus map, native scene or Room choice.
Old dated verification documents describe their recorded revision only; they do
not establish acceptance of the current towns or KayKit collection.
