## What to build

The Game distinguishes temporary Disconnect from permanent departure: disconnected Players retain their place, while Leave or expiry removes a living Player from participation, invalidates affected choices, and preserves their historical Seat and roster entry.

Each slice includes its relevant server behavior, explicit protocol contract, client UI/state, documentation, and tests. Preserve per-Room serialization, bounded asynchronous delivery, strict decoding, and frame-boundary application. Use the approved WebSocket behavior seam, focused frontend tests, and browser verification; do not defer privacy or recovery of this slice's own state to a later ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Keep disconnected living Players alive, targetable, and counted for the existing 120-second recovery reservation. Clocks and accepted choices continue unchanged.
- [ ] On intentional Leave or recovery expiry, mark a living participant forfeited and remove them from living counts. Leaving after elimination does not reduce living counts again.
- [ ] Remove the forfeiting actor's pending Mafia vote, Doctor protection, Sheriff investigation, or Meeting ballot as applicable.
- [ ] Make choices targeting the forfeiting Player ineffective without releasing the submitter's lock or allowing a replacement that phase. Temporary Disconnect does not invalidate targets.
- [ ] Use the current living roster for majority thresholds at resolution, and serialize Forfeit with deadlines, submitted actions, and recovery.
- [ ] Show an empty Seat marked Left without rearranging others. Retain original identity, Role, and completed voting history for later results; the Game Roster is not an authorization to rejoin.
- [ ] Preserve fresh-Join prohibition and remove a started Room when its final membership ends. Historical participants never keep an empty Room alive.
- [ ] Verify Leave and expiry for each Role and for a chosen target, recovery immediately before expiry, post-elimination departure, competing deadline/recovery events, and stable roster presentation.
- [ ] Make the living-roster transition available to the final victory handling in [#21](https://github.com/nirmaljb/pu-town/issues/21); this slice does not add an abort rule. Demonstrate departure/recovery in the browser, update lifecycle docs, and run full affected checks.
