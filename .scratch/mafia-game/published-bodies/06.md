## What to build

Living Players can commit a Meeting ballot or Skip, see the final attributed ballots, and watch the majority target become a spectator with a Mafia or Not Mafia reveal.

Each slice includes its relevant server behavior, explicit protocol contract, client UI/state, documentation, and tests. Preserve per-Room serialization, bounded asynchronous delivery, strict decoding, and frame-boundary application. Use the approved WebSocket behavior seam, focused frontend tests, and browser verification; do not defer privacy or recovery of this slice's own state to a later ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Allow ballots only during the 30-second voting stage, from living Players, targeting a living Player including themselves or choosing Skip.
- [ ] Use local preview and explicit confirmation. Every accepted ballot, including Skip, is final; restore its lock on Reconnect.
- [ ] Keep ballots hidden from other Players until the deadline, then reveal who voted for whom.
- [ ] Require more than half of all living Players to select the same target, including disconnected Players in the denominator. Skips and missing ballots do not lower the threshold; ties or insufficient support eliminate nobody.
- [ ] Reuse the elimination and spectator behavior introduced by [#15](https://github.com/nirmaljb/pu-town/issues/15) so a voted-out Mafia also loses living-Player permissions.
- [ ] Show the six-second result animation with Mafia or Not Mafia, never Doctor versus Sheriff, or an explicit no-elimination outcome. Continue the cycle until final victory handling is added by [#21](https://github.com/nirmaljb/pu-town/issues/21).
- [ ] Verify self-votes, Skip locks, ties, plurality without majority, disconnected counts, final disclosure, non-Mafia role secrecy, recovery, and boundary races via external behavior.
- [ ] Demonstrate a Meeting that eliminates a Mafia and one that eliminates nobody. Update affected contract/docs and run full affected checks.
